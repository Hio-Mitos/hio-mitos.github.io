AuthBox — Release history

Every public release, newest first

Each entry is the release as it was published on the Microsoft Store, dated the day it went live. Entries are never rewritten afterwards; if an earlier note turns out to be wrong, the correction appears in a later release.

1.1.1

30 September 2026

New

  • Report a problem or send feedback from inside the app (⋯ menu). AuthBox shows you the full message first and opens it in your own email program — it still sends nothing by itself, and nothing from your vault is ever included.

Improved

  • Only one master-password prompt at a time. Clicking the tray icon while AuthBox is already asking now brings that prompt to the front instead of opening another one.

Fixed

  • Dialogs no longer cut off their text on PCs with larger text settings, including the Settings, Delete profile and backup QR windows.

Documents

  • Privacy policy updated 30 September 2026 to describe the in-app Report a problem and Send feedback options.

1.0.0

15 September 2026 First release

First public release.

  • Offline TOTP and HOTP code generation — no account, no server, no network needed
  • Vault encrypted with Argon2id and AES-256-GCM under a master password you choose
  • Auto-lock: the window locks when closed, and after a period of inactivity you can adjust or switch off in Settings
  • Your master password is required again before changing security settings, making a portable copy, or exporting backup QR codes
  • Separate profiles, each with its own vault and its own master password
  • Single-file portable copy you can run from a USB stick
  • Light and dark themes that follow your Windows setting
  • Launch at startup, system tray, and global hotkeys
  • Clock drift correction for codes that keep getting rejected

Documents

  • Published with the privacy policy (effective 14 September 2026), the licence (effective 15 September 2026) and the support page.
