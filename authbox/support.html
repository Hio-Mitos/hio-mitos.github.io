AuthBox — Support

Questions, problems and recovery

Contact details are being set up and will be published here shortly.

Please never include a secret key, a recovery phrase or your master password in a support message. They are never needed to help you.

Common questions

I forgot my master password. Can you recover my vault?

No, and neither can anyone else. The vault is encrypted with a key derived from your master password, and the password itself is never stored anywhere. This is deliberate: a recovery route for you would be a recovery route for an attacker. If the password is lost, the accounts have to be re-enrolled with each service using its own recovery process.

My codes are being rejected as invalid.

This is almost always a clock problem — time-based codes only match if your system clock is accurate to within a few seconds. Use Sync time in the app, which checks the correct time over HTTPS and stores a correction offset.

Where are my files kept?

Your vaults are *.authbox files on your own computer, next to a handful of small settings files. Uninstalling AuthBox deliberately does not delete them, so reinstalling or upgrading never destroys your accounts. The privacy policy lists every file the app writes.

How do I move my accounts to another computer?

Copy the vault file across and open it with the same master password, or use the portable copy feature to carry a profile on a USB drive.

How do I delete everything?

Uninstall the app, then delete the vault files and the small plaintext settings files listed in the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

When reporting a bug, it helps to include your Windows version, the AuthBox version shown in the app, what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. Screenshots are welcome as long as they do not show a secret key or a QR code.

Questions and comments

Please read before you post

This comment box is a temporary measure while a permanent support channel is set up. It will be replaced, and comments posted here may not be carried over when that happens.

It is provided by Cusdis, an independent third-party service. It is not part of AuthBox, and it is not covered by the app's privacy policy. That policy describes the app, which collects nothing. This box is a different thing: whatever you type here is sent to and stored on Cusdis's servers rather than on your own machine, and — as with any request to any website — Cusdis will also receive ordinary technical details such as your IP address. Their handling of it is governed by their terms, not ours.

So please treat anything you post as public and outside our control:

  • Never post a secret key, a recovery phrase, a seed phrase or your master password.
  • Avoid personal details you would not want a stranger to read.
  • A name is optional — anything that identifies you is your choice to give.

Comments are reviewed before they appear, and replies are posted publicly underneath them. If a question needs private details, please wait for the permanent support channel instead of posting them here.
