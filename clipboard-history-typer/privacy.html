Clipboard History Typer – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Clipboard History Typer ("the app") is published by Hio Mitos. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app handles

The app keeps a list of the text you copy, so you can type or paste it again. Only text is kept; images, files and other clipboard content are ignored. Copies that other apps (such as password managers) mark as private are never recorded, and you can choose apps whose copies are never recorded.

Where it is stored

Your clipboard history is kept only in your PC's memory and is deleted when the app closes. Snippets you choose to pin, your settings and the app's error logs are saved on your PC in the app's settings folder. The error logs contain technical details such as error messages and times, not the text you copy.

What is sent anywhere

Nothing. The app makes no network connections, contains no analytics or advertising, and does not share any information with Hio Mitos or anyone else.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback" or "Report a problem", the app opens a new message in your own email app, addressed to hio-mitos@gladiators.city, with the app version, your Windows version and, for problem reports, the last lines of the app's error logs. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app, and you can edit or remove anything first. We use what you send only to answer you and improve the app.

Microsoft Store

When you get the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information under its own privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Close the app to delete the history. To delete pinned snippets, settings and logs, uninstall the app or delete its settings folder (Settings > Open settings folder).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
