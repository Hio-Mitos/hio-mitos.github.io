Getting started

After you start Clipboard History Typer, it runs quietly in the notification area near the clock. If you don't see it, click the ^ arrow to show hidden icons. Copy text as usual, and Clipboard History Typer keeps your recent text copies (50 by default), separate from the Windows+V history. Left-click the tray icon to open your history straight away, or right-click it for the menu.

Default shortcutWhat it does
Win + Alt + VOpen history, next to the mouse. Click an item to type it into the app you were using, or Ctrl+click to paste it normally. Start typing to search.
Ctrl + Alt + VType most recent: types the most recent item straight away.
EscStops typing at any time.

Typing text, rather than pasting it, works where pasting is blocked: remote desktops, virtual machine consoles, and apps or web forms that block pasting.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the shortcuts?

Open Settings… from the tray menu. Under Shortcuts, click the Open history or Type most recent box and press the new keys (Esc keeps the old shortcut). Press Save; the change applies straight away, with no restart.

A shortcut does nothing

Another running app may have claimed the same key combination. Close that app or change its shortcut, or set a different combination in Settings….

Nothing gets typed into an app that runs as administrator

Windows blocks normal apps from sending keystrokes to apps running as administrator, so Clipboard History Typer can't type into them. For security reasons, Windows never allows any app to type into the User Account Control (UAC) consent prompt.

Characters go missing when typing into a slow app or remote session

Slow the typing down in Settings… under Typing speed: increase Delay after each character (ms), and if needed Key down / up gap (ms) or Delay after each line break (ms). For long texts, Extra pause every (characters) and Extra pause length (ms) add a short break at regular intervals. Press Save; no restart is needed.

How do I stop certain apps from being recorded?

Open Settings… and, under Ignored apps, tick Don't record copies made in:. Then choose Add app… to pick from a list of your apps, or type one per line. Press Save. Copies that password managers mark as private are never recorded anyway. To pause recording completely, untick Monitoring enabled in the tray menu.

How do I keep more or fewer items?

Open Settings… and change History size (items) under General (50 by default), then press Save. Pinned snippets always stay at the top.

What does "Keep running" do?

With Keep running on (in Settings… under General), the app starts again by itself if it's closed unexpectedly. Restart after a stop (sec) sets how soon. When you choose Quit, the app asks whether to quit completely or close for now; Reopen after Quit (sec) sets how long "for now" lasts.

"Start with Windows" won't turn on

If startup was switched off in Task Manager or Windows Settings, Windows only lets you switch it back on there: go to Settings > Apps > Startup and switch Clipboard History Typer on. If your organization manages this setting, contact your IT administrator.

Is my clipboard history saved or sent anywhere?

Your history stays in memory on your PC and is gone when the app closes. Clear history (keeps pinned) empties it at any time. Pinned snippets, your settings and the app's error logs are saved on your PC in the app's settings folder (Settings… > Open settings folder). The app makes no network connections. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I uninstall Clipboard History Typer?

Choose Quit in the tray menu (if Keep running is on, choose Quit completely). Then go to Settings > Apps > Installed apps, find Clipboard History Typer, and choose Uninstall.

System requirements

Windows 10 or Windows 11, on a desktop PC or laptop.

Reporting a bug

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include:

  • what you were doing when it happened, and what you expected to happen;
  • the app you were typing into, if relevant;
  • the full text of any error message, or a screenshot of it.

Please don't send us passwords or other sensitive text from your clipboard.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a vulnerability, email hio-mitos@gladiators.city with the details and please hold off on publishing until it's fixed.
