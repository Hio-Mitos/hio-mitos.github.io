Clipboard History Typer

Types your copied text as real keystrokes

Clipboard History Typer remembers the text you copy and types it back for you – as real keystrokes, exactly as written.

Some places don't let you paste: remote desktops, virtual machine consoles, and apps or web forms that block pasting. Clipboard History Typer types the text instead, key by key, so it works wherever a keyboard works.

How it works

  • Copy text as usual. Clipboard History Typer keeps your recent text copies (50 by default), separate from the Windows+V history.
  • Press Win + Alt + V to open your history next to the mouse. Click an item to type it into the app you were using, or Ctrl+click to paste it normally.
  • Press Ctrl + Alt + V to type the most recent item straight away.
  • Press Esc to stop typing at any time.

Types text exactly

  • Line breaks are typed as Shift+Enter, so chat apps and forms don't send too early. Spaces, tabs, indentation and Unicode characters are kept.
  • Uses the keyboard layout of the app you type into, including dead keys on international layouts.
  • Works inside Remote Desktop, Windows App, Azure Virtual Desktop and Citrix-style sessions.
  • Adjustable typing speed for slow apps and remote connections.

Find and keep what you need

  • Start typing in the history window to search.
  • Pin snippets you use often: they stay at the top and are kept after restarts.
  • Delete single items, or clear the whole history from the tray menu.

Private by design

  • Only text is recorded; images and files are ignored.
  • Copies that password managers mark as private are never recorded.
  • Choose apps to ignore, such as your password manager, from a list of the apps on your PC.
  • Pause recording from the tray menu at any time.
  • Your history stays in memory on your PC and is gone when the app closes. The app makes no network connections.

Made to stay out of the way

  • Lives in the system tray, starts instantly and uses very little memory.
  • Optional Start with Windows.
  • Optional Keep running: if the app is closed unexpectedly, it starts again by itself. Quit lets you close it completely or just for a few seconds.
  • Set your own shortcuts by simply pressing them.
  • Light and dark mode that follows Windows, sharp at any display scaling and on every monitor.

Privacy in one line

Your history stays in memory and is gone when the app closes; pinned snippets, settings and error logs are saved only on your PC, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. The full details are in the privacy policy.
