1.3.2

12 August 2026

This update fixes 'Run at startup' so the app now reliably launches after a reboot — it wasn't actually working correctly before. Also adds:

  • Press and hold Esc to cancel typing partway through
  • Smarter handling when a shortcut you're customizing is already used by another app, or when Windows Settings has 'Run at startup' turned off — the app now tells you clearly and offers a direct way to fix it
