Clipboard Typer

Clipboard history you can type or paste

Clipboard Typer remembers your last 50 copied text items and lets you instantly reuse any of them — either typed out as simulated keystrokes or pasted directly. Everything runs on your own PC: the history lives in memory only and is gone the moment the app closes.

What it does

  • Keeps a history of your last 50 copied text items.
  • Win + Alt + V opens the history picker; Ctrl + Alt + V instantly retypes the most recent item. Both shortcuts can be changed.
  • Re-inserts an item as simulated keystrokes or as a normal paste — typing works in places where pasting is blocked.
  • Works inside Remote Desktop and Windows App sessions.
  • The picker is fully keyboard-navigable: arrow keys, Enter and Ctrl + Enter.
  • Press and hold Esc to cancel typing partway through.
  • Pause collection with Monitoring enabled, or wipe the list with Clear history, from the tray menu.
  • Optional run at startup.

Privacy in one line

Clipboard Typer never writes your clipboard history to disk and never sends anything anywhere. The full details are in the privacy policy.
