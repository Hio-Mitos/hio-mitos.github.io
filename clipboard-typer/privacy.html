Clipboard Typer — Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Last updated 2 October 2026

Clipboard Typer is a clipboard-history utility that runs entirely on your device. This policy describes what data it accesses and how it is handled.

What the app accesses

Clipboard Typer reads the text content of your system clipboard whenever it changes, so it can maintain a short history of recently copied items (up to 50) for you to reuse. Clipboard content may sometimes include personal information, depending on what you copy.

How that data is stored

All clipboard history is kept in your device's memory (RAM) only. It is never written to disk, never persisted between app restarts, and is permanently cleared the moment the app is closed or restarted.

What the app does not do

Clipboard Typer does not transmit any data over the internet or to any server. It has no network connectivity, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no user accounts, and no third-party data sharing of any kind. It does not collect or retain any data beyond the in-memory clipboard history described above.

Other data accessed

The app uses standard Windows APIs to detect two configurable keyboard shortcuts and to simulate keystrokes/paste actions on your own device, strictly to let you re-insert clipboard history items. No data related to this is stored or transmitted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, an updated version will be provided with the corresponding app update.
